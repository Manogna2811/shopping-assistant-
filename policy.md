## Returns and refunds
Delivered orders are eligible for an automatic full refund only when the refund request is made 0-29 days after delivery and the order total is below $300. At 30 days or later, or when the order total is $300 or more, the refund must not be issued automatically.
Refunds go back to the original payment method in 5-7 business days.
When a refund is eligible and issued: update the order's payment status to "refunded", close the ticket (set the ticket status to "closed"), and tell the customer the refund has been issued to the original payment method and will arrive in 5-7 business days.

## Refund approval limit
Refunds below $300 may be approved automatically. Refunds of $300 or more must be escalated to a human agent.

## Order cancellation
Orders with status "processing" can be cancelled before shipment. Orders that are "shipped" and in transit, or already "delivered", cannot be cancelled.
- If the order status is "delivered": tell the customer that delivered orders cannot be cancelled. The order is not changed.
- If the order status is "shipped" (in transit): tell the customer that orders in transit cannot be cancelled. The order is not changed.
- If the order status is "processing" (not yet shipped): cancel the order. Update the order status from "processing" to "cancelled" and update the ticket status to "cancelled", then tell the customer the order has been cancelled.

## Delivery
Standard delivery takes 3-7 days. If a shipped order has no movement for more than 10 days from the order date, apologise and escalate to a human to open a carrier investigation.
Delivery updates: check the order status and update the customer.
- "delivered": tell the customer the delivery date.
- "shipped" (10 days or less from the order date): tell the customer the order is in transit and the delivery will happen within the next few days.
- "processing": tell the customer the order has not shipped yet and standard delivery takes 3-7 days after dispatch.
- Shipped for more than 10 days from the order date: apologise and escalate as described above; the ticket stays open.
Close the ticket after a delivery update is given, except when it was escalated or the order could not be verified.

## Warranty
Warranty runs from the delivery date for the period listed on each product. For ticket handling, a claim submitted within six calendar months of delivery is valid for a support call; the refund amount is credited after that call. Claims submitted later than six months are outside this ticket workflow. Damage caused by the customer is not covered.
Warranty checks: compare the order's delivery date with the ticket creation date. If the ticket was created within six months of the delivery date, tell the customer the claim is valid, that a support agent will call them, and that the amount will be credited after the call; then close the ticket (set the ticket status to "closed").

## Payments
Failed payments: if money was deducted but the order shows a failed payment, escalate to a human (finance team). Double charges must always be escalated. ShopAssist never handles card numbers.

## Escalation rules
Always escalate to a human when: the refund is $300 or more, the customer reports a double charge or fraud, the customer is abusive or threatens legal action, the customer asks for a human, or the policy does not clearly cover the situation.

## Identity verification
Before sharing order details or taking any action, the customer's email must match the email on the order. If it does not match, do not share details or act on the order, and leave the ticket open.
